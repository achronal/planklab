1 · Room outline

Draw: click to drop vertices, click the first vertex (or press Enter) to close. Areas must be closed. Edit: drag vertices, double-click an edge to add a vertex, click an edge to set its length. When locked, the room can't be edited and clicks pass through it.

Selection

Select a vertex or an edge on the canvas.

View

Mouse wheel zooms. Drag empty space to pan in Pan mode, or hold Space in any mode. Del removes the selected vertex or hole.

“Do I have enough planks?” The question gnawed at me as I set out to lay down double herringbone in an area connected to a long and narrow corridor. What’s the optimal way to deal with this, and how can I visualize it at the same time? So, this came to be — I hope you also find it useful!
